ecdh.chat for your computer

The same private chat as a small, fast desktop app for Windows and Linux. It carries its own code and keeps your chat open in the tray.

  • [WIN]Windows

    10 and 11 · 64-bit · – · –

    Download for Windows

    Run the installer. The app isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you: choose More info, then Run anyway.

    SHA-256

    Get-FileHash $HOME\Downloads\ecdh.chat-setup.exe

  • [LNX]Linux · .deb

    Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 and newer · – · –

    Download .deb

    Open it with your software installer, or run:

    sudo apt install ./ecdh.chat_amd64.deb

    SHA-256

    sha256sum ecdh.chat_amd64.deb

  • [LNX]Linux · AppImage

    Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13 and newer · – · –

    Download AppImage

    Make it executable and start it. It needs FUSE 2 (libfuse2t64).

    chmod +x ecdh.chat-x86_64.AppImage./ecdh.chat-x86_64.AppImage

    SHA-256

    sha256sum ecdh.chat-x86_64.AppImage

Calls aren’t in the Linux app yet; for calls, open ecdh.chat in a browser.

What the app adds

  • [01]

    Code that ships with the app

    What runs on your computer only changes when you update.

  • [02]

    Privacy blur and screenshot blocking

    Blur the chat when you look away. On Windows, hide it from screenshots.

  • [03]

    Stays with you

    Your chat stays open in the tray, with alerts that never show what was said.

  • [04]

    Tiny and light

    Under 2 MB on Windows, about 4 MB as a .deb.
