Reporting a security issue

We take reports seriously and will work with you to fix problems quickly.

What to include

  • What you found and what an attacker could do with it
  • Steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept
  • The app version or the date you tested the website

What we ask

  • Test only with chats and devices you control
  • Don't try to read other people's chats, and don't overload the service
  • Give us a reasonable time to fix it before telling others

What you can expect

  • A reply, usually within a few days
  • Updates while we work on a fix
  • Credit for the find, if you'd like it

For something sensitive, email us first and we'll set up an ecdh.chat conversation, so the details never sit in an inbox.
