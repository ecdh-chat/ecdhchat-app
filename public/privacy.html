Privacy policy

We built ecdh.chat to know as little as possible.

This page explains what little we do handle, why, and for how long. Last updated 19 October 2026.

The short version

We can't read your chats

Messages, files, voice notes and calls are encrypted on your device and go straight to the other person. They never reach our servers in a form we could read, and we never store them.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-up, no names, no phone numbers. No cookies, analytics, ads or third-party scripts. We don't sell or share data, because we don't collect it.

Your IP address, briefly

Connecting two devices needs your IP address. Our server uses it while you connect and to stop abuse, keeps it only in memory, and doesn't log it.

Who runs ecdh.chat

ecdh.chat is run by its developer, reachable at god@ecdh.chat. For privacy questions or requests, write to hello@ecdh.chat.

What we handle, and why

Everything below is processed only as long as it's needed, and none of it is written to a database or a log of individual chats.

Your chatsNever. Contents are end-to-end encrypted and travel directly between the two devices. See how security works.
IP addressWhen you open the website or start a chat. Used to deliver the page, introduce the two devices and limit how many chats one network can start. Kept in memory while you're connected, and the abuse counters are dropped within about a minute.
Connection setupNetwork addresses and encryption fingerprints the two devices exchange to connect. Passed straight through and not stored.
Waiting roomsA random room name, kept in memory until the other person joins or for 30 minutes at most.
Relay (Hide my IP)If the relay is used, it sees both IP addresses and how much encrypted data flows while the chat lasts. It can't see the contents and doesn't keep a record afterwards.
DownloadsThe installer is served without logging who downloads it.
Email to usIf you email one of our addresses, we receive your address and message, and keep them as long as needed to reply. Ask us to delete them any time.

On your device

Your browser or the app remembers a few preferences, such as the theme, sounds, notifications and Hide my IP. The desktop app also keeps its settings in a small file on your computer. These never leave your device and contain nothing about your chats. Clear them with your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the app and choosing to delete its data. On Linux, delete the app's chat.ecdh.desktop folders in ~/.config and ~/.local/share.

Companies that help us run it

Hetzner OnlineHosts our server in Helsinki, Finland (EU). Website, connection setup and relay traffic pass through it.
CloudflareRuns the domain's DNS and forwards email sent to our addresses. Website and chat traffic don't go through Cloudflare. Cloudflare is a US company and may process forwarded email outside the EU under its data processing terms.
Let's EncryptIssues our HTTPS certificate. It receives no information about visitors.

Legal basis and your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process the little data above on the basis of our legitimate interests: running a secure, working service and preventing abuse (Article 6(1)(f)). You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or object to its use. Because we don't keep data linked to you, there's usually nothing to hand over, but we'll always answer. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
